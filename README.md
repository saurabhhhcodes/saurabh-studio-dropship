## Zero-Inventory Dropshipping Model
1. **Upfront Customer Checkout**: Customer orders online and pays full retail price ($27.99 - $69.99 / ₹1,699 - ₹5,799) via UPI (`saurabhbajpai03@okhdfcbank`) or international card.
2. **Instant Fund Settlement**: 100% of retail payment enters Saurabh Bajpai's verified account (HDFC Bank / UPI). Zero capital out-of-pocket.
3. **Automated Print-on-Demand Routing**: Order is transmitted to Gelato or Printify hubs (130+ facilities across 32 countries). Wholesale manufacturing cost ($8.90 - $22.50) is deducted from the collected payment.
4. **Direct Doorstep Dropship**: Supplier prints, inspects, packages under Saurabh Studio branding, and ships via FedEx / DHL / BlueDart / USPS directly to the buyer's home address.
5. **Net Profit Retained**: Saurabh keeps a clean **58% - 71.5% net profit margin** ($19.09 - $50.09 / ₹959 - ₹4,149 per item) on every order.
